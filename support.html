Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change the color of dimension lines or badges?

EcomKit inserts graphics as native vector SVGs. Once an element is placed onto your canvas, select it and click Canva's standard color palette icons in the top toolbar to apply your preferred brand colors.

Do I need a separate EcomKit account or subscription?

No. EcomKit operates directly within your existing Canva editor workspace. There is no external registration, third-party password, or separate user account required.

What should I do if an element fails to insert?

Make sure you have an active design page open in Canva. If you receive an error, refresh your browser tab and try clicking the element again. If the issue persists, please reach out to our support email with your browser details.

Where can I view the source code and updates?

You can track project updates, report issues, and review releases on our public GitHub repository at github.com/zhizhuzhizhu007/ecomkit.
