Core Features

Two focused tools designed specifically for product listings, infographics, and promotional graphics.

↔

Dimension Lines

Generate vector dimension callouts to display product sizes accurately on e-commerce images.

  • Orientation: Horizontal or vertical lines.
  • Measurement Units: cm, in, mm, px, m, and quotation marks (").
  • End Styles: Double arrows, single arrows, ticks, and round dots.
  • Adjustable Stroke: Thin (1.5px), Medium (2.5px), or Thick (4.0px).
  • Label Background: Toggle the text capsule on or off for clean line aesthetics.
  • Native Color Adaptation: Directly inserted as native vector SVGs, editable via Canva's color toolbar.
✓

Trust Badges

Insert clean, focused trust badges into product cards, banners, and store graphics.

  • Three Design Styles: Minimalist Line, Solid Pill, and Classic Shield.
  • Four Key Themes: Free Shipping, Secure Payment, 100% Organic, and 30-Day Return.
  • Two-Color Maximum: Designed to maintain visual hierarchy and palette cohesion.
  • One-Click Insertion: Simply click any badge to place it onto the active page.
  • Fully Recolor-Ready: Pure SVG structure compatible with Canva's palette tools.

How EcomKit Works

EcomKit runs entirely within Canva's Design Editor environment:

Step 1

Open in Canva

Launch EcomKit from the Canva Apps side panel inside your design project.

Step 2

Configure or Select

Set dimension values and line styles, or browse trust badge collections by style.

Step 3

Add to Design

Insert vector elements onto your canvas instantly and adjust colors as needed.

Transparency & Scope

EcomKit is focused strictly on graphic generation within your Canva workspace:

  • Operates as a client-side Canva application using the official Canva Apps SDK.
  • Does not operate a standalone third-party user account or subscription database.
  • Vector assets are generated locally and inserted directly into your Canva page.
